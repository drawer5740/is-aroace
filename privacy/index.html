Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how is-aroace handles information when you request or use a subdomain, sign in, or manage DNS records.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Account information: your email address and account identifiers used by the authentication service.
  • Subdomain requests: the name you request, the name you provide, your email address, the purpose you describe, an optional preview URL, request status, and review timestamps or notes.
  • DNS data: host names, record types, record values, TTLs, and operational details required to manage the DNS configuration you submit. DNS records are generally public and can be queried by others.
  • Technical and security information: session data in browser storage and information processed by service providers for security, diagnostics, abuse prevention, and reliability. Providers may process technical data such as IP address, browser details, and request logs.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, review subdomain requests, assign or remove subdomains, operate DNS management, respond to support or abuse reports, protect the service, troubleshoot failures, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database, and application backend services. deSEC provides DNS management for the records that the service writes. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services under their own policies and agreements. This site does not control their independent operations.

4. Browser storage

The authentication client stores session information in browser storage so you can remain signed in and refresh your session. Clearing site storage may sign you out.

5. Sharing

Account and request details may be visible to authorized service administrators who review requests and maintain the service. Information is sent to the providers described above when required to provide the service. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to address abuse, protect rights or safety, or comply with a lawful request. The operator should not sell personal information.

6. Retention and deletion

Information is kept for as long as needed to operate accounts, review requests, manage subdomain assignments, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Deleting an account or subdomain may remove application records and DNS records as described in the service. Backups, provider logs, and security records may persist for a limited period according to provider retention practices.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect the service. No website, internet transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not place passwords, private keys, access tokens, or other secrets in DNS records, request descriptions, or preview links.

8. Your choices

You can choose whether to provide an optional preview link, or submit a subdomain request. You may manage assigned subdomains through account settings when those functions are available. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. The operator may need to retain some information for security, operational, or legal reasons.

9. Children and other users

The service is not intended to collect more personal information than needed to operate accounts and review requests. Users should not submit another person's personal information without a valid reason and authorization.

10. International processing

Supabase and deSEC (used to host the site) may process data in locations where they or their infrastructure operate. Applicable protections and transfer rules depend on the providers, their configuration, and your location.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service or its providers change. The “Last updated” date above indicates the most recent revision.

12. Privacy questions and requests

Contact the service operator through the contact channel published by the site owner to request access, correction, deletion, or raise a privacy concern. A monitored privacy contact address should be published before this policy is used in production.
